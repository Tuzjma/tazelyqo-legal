Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

This policy explains what data the game Tazelyqo Tower (“the app”, “we”) uses, why, and what choices you have. We do not ask for your name, e-mail address, phone number, contacts, location, camera or microphone.

Cloud save (your game progress)

So that you never lose your progress, the app automatically saves your game progress to our server and restores it after a reinstall or on a new phone. The cloud save contains:

  • Game progress: level, stars, coins, solved-word statistics, collected cards, chosen languages and game settings.
  • A random save code created by the app (not linked to your identity).
  • A one-way hash of the device's app-specific Android ID. The raw ID never leaves your phone; the hash only lets the same phone find its progress again after a reinstall.
  • If you are signed in to Google Play Games: a one-way hash of your Play Games player ID, so your progress follows your Google account to a new phone. We do not receive your name, e-mail address or profile picture.

The server is operated for us by our hosting provider Railway (servers in the United States). Data is sent over encrypted HTTPS. We keep a cloud save while it is in use; earlier versions of a save (up to 50) are kept so a mistaken overwrite can be undone. Requests are protected by rate limiting; IP addresses are not stored in the database.

Google Play Games Services

The app uses Google Play Games Services to sign you in automatically with the Google account on your device. This sign-in is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. You can turn off or delete your Play Games profile in the Google Play Games settings at any time.

Advertising

The app shows ads through Google AdMob. Ads are requested with the settings for child-directed treatment and for users under the age of consent, and with a G content rating, so ads are not personalized based on interests. AdMob may still process device and usage information (for example the advertising ID, IP address and ad interactions) to show ads, limit frequency and prevent fraud. Users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent through Google's consent message. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

In-app purchases

Optional purchases (coin packs and “No ads” passes) are processed by Google Play. We never receive your payment details; Google tells the app only which item was bought. Parents can require approval for purchases with Google Play parental controls.

Children

The app is designed to be safe for young learners: no chat, no user-generated content, no sharing with other players, no personal data requested, and ads are limited as described above.

Your choices and deleting your data

You can ask us to delete your cloud save at any time by writing to tanatata434@gmail.com. Please tell us roughly when you last played; if you can, include the Google account you use for Play Games so we can find the save. Uninstalling the app removes the local copy from your phone.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app changes. The current version is always available at this address.
