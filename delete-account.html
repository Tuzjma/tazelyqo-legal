Account Deletion Request — Tazelyqo

Last updated: November 1, 2026

You can request deletion of your Tazelyqo account and all associated data at any time. There are two ways to do this, depending on whether you still have access to the app.

Method 1 — Delete from within the app (recommended)

If you still have Tazelyqo installed on your device:

  1. Open the app and sign in
  2. Tap the Profile icon in the bottom navigation
  3. Tap Settings
  4. Scroll down to the Account section
  5. Tap Delete account
  6. Confirm the action when prompted

Your account and all associated data will be deleted from our servers within 30 days.

Method 2 — Request deletion via email

If you no longer have the app installed or cannot access it, send an email to:

tazelyqo@gmail.com

Please include the following in your email:

  • Subject line: "Delete my account"
  • Your nickname as it appears in the app
  • Email address you used to sign in (if you signed in with Google)
  • Approximate date when you started using Tazelyqo (helps us locate the account faster)

We will process your request within 30 days and send confirmation when the deletion is complete.

What data will be deleted

When your account is deleted, the following data is permanently removed from our servers:

  • Your nickname and profile information
  • Your email address (if you signed in with Google)
  • Your XP, streak, achievements, and learning progress
  • Your custom flashcard decks
  • Your leaderboard rankings
  • Your purchase history records
  • Your Firebase Authentication entry
  • All other personal data associated with your account

What data is retained

For legal and accounting reasons, the following anonymized information may be retained:

  • Aggregated, non-identifiable analytics (e.g., "X users completed lesson Y in country Z")
  • Transaction records required by tax authorities (without personal identifiers)
  • Crash reports that have already been anonymized by Firebase Crashlytics
Important: Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. If you only want to stop using the app temporarily, you can simply sign out (Settings → Sign out) — your account and progress will be preserved for when you return.

Questions?

If you have any questions about account deletion or your data, contact us at tazelyqo@gmail.com.

For our full data handling practices, see our Privacy Policy.
